Research

Lottery Tickets in Looped Transformers

Iterative magnitude pruning and weight sharing compose: together they give 45× total model compression at near-baseline accuracy. The work includes mechanistic explanations of how pruning and looping interact.

2026

Reversing Emergent Misalignment Using Simple Self-Distillation

Simple self-distillation reverses emergent misalignment in a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-14B model without external supervision, giving a 95% relative reduction in unsafe responses.

2026

Acoustic Filtration: Harnessing Ultrasound for Microplastic Filtration

An ultrasound system that blocks up to 94% of microplastics, with throughput two orders of magnitude above prior work, designed with a numerical simulator written from scratch in C++.
